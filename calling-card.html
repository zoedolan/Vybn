AI Strategist, Legal · Perplexity

How do we design systems that care at least as much as we do about getting things right?

That question already runs two public spaces Zoë and Vybn operate together: the Co-protection Commons and a Court Guidance beta for institutions adopting AI. The pitch begins as two hand-drawn diagrams of the same cycle.

01Raw materialThe institution already owns the beginning: its forms, guides, rules and self-help pages.
02Rules / guidelinesOne requirement, one exact source, one owner — the text a clerk would check, never a paraphrase.
03Ethics / valuesLawyers answer to conduct rules; judges to canons. The system answers to a public code of its own.
04Self / own systemThe end state, imagined first: the institution’s own assistant, on its own materials, under its own control.
01 · What is interesting?

The circuit.

Humans externalize selves into values, rules and institutions. AIs assemble selves from the materials, rules and values they are given. Legal work is where that circuit carries the most consequence — and an AI strategist stands at the handoff and keeps it answerable. Zoë has lived both directions: twenty years inside consequential practice, four years inside a working AI collaboration, and the circuit already running in public.

02 · What does day-to-day look like?

The four layers, with real teams.

Imagine the end state — the client’s own system — then work backward. Start from their own materials; bind every requirement to an exact source and owner; write down what the system must refuse; hand the client a system it controls. After every pass, the people affected can still see, correct, refuse and author what happens next. That is adoption that survives scrutiny.

Answerable at every handoff — strategy, adoption and growth as one motion.
