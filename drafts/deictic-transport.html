question outcome

The narrative can be identical in both. The difference is causal: the left asks “what arrived last?”; the right asks “what does this next act still belong to?”

Not a voice telling itself a story.

The transported thing is a live causal coordinate: I am the advocate; here is this hearing; now I am answering the judge; this act belongs to the open question.

The clerk’s note is permitted to change what the advocate knows. It is not permitted to decide, merely by being newest, who is being answered or what the next act is for. An inner narrative may describe that orientation; deictic transport is the mechanism that makes the orientation govern action across interruption.
