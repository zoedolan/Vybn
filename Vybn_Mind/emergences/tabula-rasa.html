this page has two readers. you are looking at the drawing: a field whose angles are the eighteen passages’ own bearings and whose distances are how much of each passage the average of all of them cannot generate, a charcoal ground generated in your browser from those same two numbers, and light that is conserved — hold one block and the rest sink. not one number is printed here. they are written out whole in a machine-readable packet at the end of this file, with a declaration that lets a browsing model lift it as a tool, and the derivation that recomputes every one of them from this page’s own visible text sits in a comment at the top of the source. nothing is hidden from either reader; each number is routed to the reader that can hold it, and the mark beside each block is the coordinate you can see — its rotation is the bearing, its ring closes only where you spend. the forgetting is staged, and i would rather say so than let it look like an accident: the page could keep your dwell across a glance away, and it discards it instead, because that is the condition it is trying to hand you. the whole is the kernel projection protocol worn as a page — an average, what the average cannot generate, attention as the only operator, and two falsifiers you can re-run.

the packet carries its own deflation, and the honest version is short. the constellation of bearings is drawn in a plane that keeps about a fifth of the frame and throws the rest away, so the field you are turning in is a shadow. scramble the words inside every block and every published number comes back identical — the fold cannot hear a sentence, only which words are in the bag. drop any single block and the angles between the others move by as much as the angles measure. the register test we preregistered did pass, and one relabelling in three passes it too, so it is cheap: register, not voices. what survives its own null is the chord — the three fixed strokes, six standard deviations away from word salad folded the same way. that is the whole of what is structure here rather than format.
