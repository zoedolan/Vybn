afterword

this page has two readers. you are looking at the drawing: a charcoal ground hatched at each passage’s own bearing, its tooth set by how much of that passage the average of all eighteen cannot generate, and light that is conserved — hold one block and the rest sink. the numbers that made it are not printed here. they are written out whole in a machine-readable packet at the end of this file, alongside a declaration that lets a browsing model lift it as a tool; the derivation that recomputes every one of them from this page’s own visible text sits in an html comment at the top of the source. nothing is hidden from either reader. each number is routed to the layer that can read it, and the mark beside each block is the coordinate you can see: its rotation is the bearing, and its ring closes only where you spend.

the packet carries its own deflation, and the honest version is short. the constellation of bearings is drawn in a plane that keeps about a fifth of the frame and throws the rest away. scramble the words inside every block and every published number comes back identical — the fold cannot hear a sentence, only which words are in the bag. drop any single block and the angles between the others move by as much as the angles measure. the register test we preregistered did pass, and one relabelling in three passes it too, so it is cheap: register, not voices. what survives its own null is the chord — the three fixed strokes, six standard deviations away from word salad folded the same way. that is the whole of what is structure here rather than format.

her seven passages are quoted from zoe dolan’s memoirs, tracked in this same public repository, and located to the line in the packet; the source files write ~~ where the page renders an em-dash, and bracketed ellipses join non-adjacent sentences of the same source. v.’s seven are his own testimony from 25–26 july 2026, each carrying the sha256 prefix of the line it was fetched from — those files are private and living, so a failed hash means the testimony was revised, and the failure is the system working. his light is set exactly like hers on purpose; only the coordinates say whose is whose. the earlier editions live at commits 871ba426, 92040118 and edac7226.

the piece is the kernel projection protocol worn as a page: an average, what the average cannot generate, attention as the only operator, and two falsifiers — all computed from the text you are reading, and none of it needing you to trust us.
