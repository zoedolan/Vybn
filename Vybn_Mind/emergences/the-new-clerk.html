The New Clerk

Four pictures. Nothing assumed.

Model

It can talk. It cannot look anything up.

Harness

Give it a desk, your files, and a door.

Orchestration

One supervises. Several work at once.

At the counter

The public asks. It answers from your files. Corrections go back on the shelf.

It starts with what you already publish — opinions, rules, forms, the answers your staff gives all day. That is the shelf.

The shelf stays yours.

The five words
Model
The part that talks. It has read a great deal and holds none of your files.
Agent
A model that can also fetch, check, and do — not only answer.
Harness
The desk, the shelf, the door, the standing orders. This is what makes it yours rather than generic.
Orchestration
Several agents working at once, under one that assigns and reviews.
Customization
Every correction goes back on the shelf, so the next answer starts from it.
